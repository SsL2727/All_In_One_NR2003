Caution — the field races back to the line
The yellow comes out three quarters of the way through the stage's last lap, the stage result is taken, and the field races back to the line before the restart.
Caution — the field stays frozen in order
Same caution, but the cars hold their positions instead of racing back.
No caution — the race stays green
Stages without cautions: the stage ends when the leader takes the line under green, the order at that moment is the stage result (points and the scorebug still update), and nobody has to stop racing.
End a stage early on a late caution
A caution with or fewer laps left in a stage ends that stage on the next lap instead of throwing a second caution. Applies from the next caution.

One car per driver
Before each race the AI roster you picked in NR2003 is cut down to one car for every driver, so the same driver cannot start in two cars. The driver you race as gets no AI car. Your roster file is put back when you switch this off or close All In One NR2003.
