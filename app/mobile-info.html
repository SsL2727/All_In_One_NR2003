All In One NR2003 — broadcast control

Driver Info

  • Driver banner. Edit Info 1 and Info 2, the two lines shown in the driver banner, for every driver.
  • Live preview. The preview shows the banner as it looks on air. Pick a driver or tick Follow camera to track the car on screen.
  • Instant. Changes go live in the broadcast at once. Clear one driver or all of them with the buttons below.
  • Value shown. Choose what appears beside each driver: gap to the leader, points, championship position, race position, gap to the cutoff line (points), or the current race position of the drivers closest to the cutoff line, kept in championship order (position).
  • Drivers. Tick up to 7. With none ticked the panel chooses for you: the top 5, or with a Cutoff Line option the 3 above and the 3 below the line.
  • Cutoff Line (points) and Cutoff Line (position) only appear while the cutoff line is on in the Championship screen of the app.
  • Live race table. Position, start position, interval, last and best lap, pits and laps led for every car.
  • Driver card. Click a driver to open his card with his season stats.
