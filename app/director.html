Camera library and editor

Cameras 22-28 are RaceVision's additions (In-car right, Drone, Side tracking, Low rear, Front quarter, Overhead, Pack view) and 29-40 are your own: each is one of the game's cameras with its placement changed in memory while it shows (and put back when another camera is on). Pick a camera, change it while you watch it on TV, then save it, or save it as a new camera. Cockpit 1 accepts turn, tilt, roll and lens values; the Drone has guarded near-clip, follow, and sound adjustments.
"RaceVision default" puts every setting and camera back; "JT's Customs" loads that preset with its camera library.

Crash replays

Crashes are found from the cars' damage and replayed with the game's own replay, from up to three angles, each starting just before that car's hardest hit. The camera hook has to be installed (read-only test mode off). Ctrl+F7 shows the replay of the crash most worth it; while you drive it pauses the race, plays it and resumes. Getting out of your car skips the game's own climb-out replay.

Playback

LIVE
NR2003's own replay controls: scrub the tape, step a frame, slow motion down to 1/17, fast forward and rewind, jump to a lap or a crash. From live it starts the tape paused a few seconds back; a replay already rolling is taken over (it then ends when you press Live). Also on the phone remote.

Dynamic cameras (test)

The director puts its own cameras where the story is - the pit entry as the leaders come in, the stall, the race off pit road, a new angle on every crash, low at the restart, the line at the finish - and learns each track as it goes. A dynamic camera is one of the game's pit lane cameras (17 / 18) moved to its place and aimed at the car, and put back when the shot ends. It needs the track's map (Race Control draws it from the cars in the first lap of a session) and the camera hook (read-only test mode off).
The test puts a camera 24 m from the car on air for 8 s. If it looks off to one side, or up when it should look down, use the aim fixes. A thumbs up or down counts three times in the angle's score.

Camera sets

Choose what NR2003's TV cameras show on a track, and the car cameras (bumper, roof, door, sides) of a series. Use now writes the set into the cameras the game is using this moment, so the next shot already shows it; Save for next session writes it over the game's file, after keeping the game's own copy, so it can always be put back. Sets are RaceVision's recipes made from the game's own cameras, and any .cam files in the camsets folder (presets\track\<track>, presets\series\<series>), in the game's own binary form or RaceVision's text form. Preview plays a simulated race through a track's set, with dynamic cameras if you like, before you pick it.
