🎨 Dynamic Rubber

— —
Tire rubber builds up on every car's body paint as a race goes on: the front bumper, the leading edge of the hood and the lower sides. It is a ReShade add-on that follows the race through NR2003's replay telemetry. It works with standard car paints (Cup, GNS, CTS) and finds new carsets when NR2003 starts. Saved replays don't rebuild the rubber from an earlier race.

Each install keeps the files it replaced in NR2003 Dynamic Rubber Backups. Undo removes the files it added and puts the old ones back; in core.ini only the two replay telemetry values go back, so game settings you changed since are kept.
NR2003 Dynamic Rubber: a community-made add-on. It uses ReShade and d3d8to9 by crosire (Patrick Mours), downloaded from their official pages when needed and checked against known hashes, and Mark Adler's blast decompressor. Their notices are in beta\dynamic-rubber\Third-party notices.txt.
