How it works

Qualifying is run in the practice session just before qualifying, with the AI cars held in their pit boxes. You can sit it out and watch on the TV cameras, or take your own turn: you are in the order, the game pauses for you, and your lap counts. In the qualifying session after it, every lap the AI post is replaced with these laps, so the leaderboard and the starting grid follow them. If you take a run, your own lap is written there too. Let the qualifying session run (or sim it to the end); don't skip it. A car that doesn't pull out in time, or wrecks on its run, is a DNQ and starts behind every car with a time.

Format

the out lap never counts
ranked by the average of consecutive timed laps; every lap counts, a run that isn't finished gets no time
Green gap: every box is a different distance from pit exit, so each car's release is timed from its own predicted out lap (learned from the cars already run) to take the green about N seconds after the car before ends its last timed lap. The first pair can be late.
a car on its in-lap counts until it reaches pit road
car numbers, space or comma separated; the rest are drawn after them. Empty = random draw.
cars without a lap go to the other end in a random draw
25:24, 10:12, 5:12 = 25 minutes, top 24 go through; 10 minutes, top 12; 5 minutes for pole. Grid: the last round by its times, then each earlier round's other cars. Practice needs about — minutes.
Group 1 and Group 2 run in turn; the fastest of each go to the Fast 12, the fastest of that to the Fast Six for pole. Grid from 13th: Group 1 on the odd spots, Group 2 on the even ones. Practice needs about — minutes.
min, up toruns a car
only with the re-qualifying lanes

Saved results (QualControl)

Save this weekend's qualifying (each car's laps as they go to the game), then reapply it at a later weekend - other weather, other time of day - and the qualifying session writes the same grid exactly. Cars are matched by their .car file, else by car number; the player's car is always the player.

Merge sessions

Several qualifying sessions (two groups, two rosters) into one order: the round reached, then the time; a driver's best counts; ties go to the earlier session. Then the race field (the cut), a merged results file to reapply, and a race roster .lst.
