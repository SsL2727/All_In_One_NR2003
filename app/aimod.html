Stages

Stages are set once, in the Stage Racing window (or by picking a real race there). AIMOD plans its stage-break stops to those lap numbers whenever Stage Racing is on; the stage fields in a preset are only used when it is off. Stage Racing's "Stages off" means no stage breaks at all - and for the AIMOD 2.2 crew chief the pre-stage era (stretch runs, no free stage-break tire stop).
